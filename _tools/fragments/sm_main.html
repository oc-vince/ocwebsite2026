Boosted posts

Your fans may not
see your posts.

Boosted social posts are a great way to target social media audiences likely to be interested in your content but who don't currently follow you. These can be set for a duration and budget that suits your needs.

One of the best reasons to boost posts is to get them in front of more eyes and overcome the decline in Facebook's ‘organic reach’. In short, your fans may not see posts or updates unless you boost them.

Providing they're engaging and relevant to your audience, boosted posts can reach more people than a regular ad. We'll set up targeting for an existing audience, or help you reach a new set of people by way of location, age, gender and interests.

Who sees the post

Unboosted organic post Your followers, some of them
Regular ad A targeted audience
Boosted, engaging post Followers plus lookalike interest

Illustrative only — actual reach depends on your audience, creative and budget. The point is the shape: providing they're engaging and relevant, boosted posts can reach more people than a regular ad.

The basics

What is social media marketing?

Talk it through with us →

Social media marketing is a type of digital marketing used to promote your organisation's products and services online.

It's achieved by creating and sharing content on social media platforms, including Facebook, LinkedIn and Instagram.

This content can be in the form of organic posts to appeal to your existing social media followers, or ads to reach new people.
